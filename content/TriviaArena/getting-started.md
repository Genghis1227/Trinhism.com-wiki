# Getting Started with Trivia Arena

This guide will walk you through setting up your teams, choosing a game format, setting match pacing with the Duration Estimator, and launching your first game.

---

## 1. Accessing the Arena

Trivia Arena requires no installation, software downloads, or user accounts.

1. Open your desktop or laptop web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Safari recommended).
2. Navigate to [`https://trinhism.com/app/trivia/`](https://trinhism.com/app/trivia/).
3. The app loads directly into the **Setup Lobby**. All settings and teams are stored locally in your browser.

> 💡 **TV Casting Tip**: For the best living room party experience, connect your laptop to a TV via HDMI or wireless screen mirroring (Chromecast / AirPlay), then press **F11** to toggle full-screen mode.

---

## 2. Configuring the Teams Roster

In the left column of the Setup Lobby, configure your competing teams:

1. **Add / Remove Teams**: Trivia Arena supports between **2 and 6 competing teams**. Click **+ Add Team** or the trash icon to adjust the roster.
2. **Assign Team Names**: Enter creative names (e.g., *"Quiztopher Walken"*, *"The Smartinis"*, *"Ctrl+Alt+Defeat"*).
3. **Select Team Colors**: Each team is assigned a distinctive high-visibility neon accent:
   * 🟦 **Cyan**
   * 🟥 **Rose**
   * 🟨 **Amber**
   * 🟩 **Emerald**
   * 🟪 **Violet**
   * 🟧 **Orange**

The system automatically tracks scores, current answer streaks, total steals, and fastest buzzer times independently for every team.

---

## 3. Selecting Your Game Mode

Under **Game Mode & Pacing**, choose between two formats:

| Mode | Format | Flow & Pacing | Best Suited For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grid Board (Jeopardy-Style)** | 6 Categories × 5 Point Rows ($100 to $500) | Teams take turns selecting any open tile from the board. Tile point values correlate with question difficulty. | Classic team trivia night, high strategy, board clearing |
| **Linear Gauntlet** | Fixed sequence of rounds | Every team answers a set number of sequential questions with escalating stakes. | Quick games, strict time constraints, tournament brackets |

---

## 4. Managing Categories & Question Decks

Trivia Arena gives you complete flexibility over where questions come from:

1. **Default Offline Deck (Built-in)**:
   * Pre-packaged with **36 verified, multi-category trivia questions**.
   * Completely offline ready. Ideal when internet connectivity is spotty or when you want to jump right in.
2. **Custom AI Categories**:
   * If you configure an AI API key (see [AI Provider Setup](/wiki/triviaarena/ai-providers/)), you can type in any custom topics you want!
   * *Examples*: *"80s Action Cinema"*, *"World Capitals & Geography"*, *"Computer Science Trivia"*, *"Gordon Ramsay & Fine Dining"*.
   * Toggle between presets (Pop Culture, Science & Nature, History, Gaming) or define your own.

---

## 5. Dialing in Pacing with the Dynamic Duration Estimator

One of Trivia Arena's standout features is its **Dynamic Game Duration Estimator** located in the right-hand panel:

* As you adjust your **Turn Timer** (e.g., 20 seconds vs 30 seconds), **Steal Window** (e.g., 8 seconds), **Number of Teams**, and **Categories**, the estimator continuously recalculates the total projected runtime.
* It displays estimated minutes for:
  * **Question Reading & Discussion Time**
  * **Turn Deliberation Window**
  * **Steal Execution Opportunities**
  * **Celebration & Leaderboard Transitions**
* Target your game night's exact schedule (e.g., dial in a crisp 35-minute match before dinner).

---

## 6. Audio Calibration & Launching the Match

1. **Test Audio Engine**: Click the **Audio** toggle in the top-right header to verify procedural sound effects are enabled. Click the test chime icon to calibrate volume.
2. **Export Setup Backup (Optional)**: Click **Export Roster (.json)** to download your customized team roster and category selections for future game nights.
3. **Launch Match**: Click the prominent **Start Game** button. The arena transitions instantly into the active game board!
