# Getting Started with 401(k) Optimizer

The **401(k) Max & Bonus Calibration Engine** is a client-side web application designed to help you reach the IRS 401(k) elective deferral limit smoothly across every paycheck while capturing 100% of your company's matching contributions.

---

## 🛠️ Prerequisites & Access

*   **Platform**: Any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) on desktop, tablet, or mobile.
*   **Web Location**: Access directly at [trinhism.com/app/401k/](https://trinhism.com/app/401k/).
*   **No Installation Required**: The application runs 100% locally in your browser with zero plugins or downloads.
*   **Data Privacy**: All financial numbers remain strictly in your browser's local cache (`localStorage`). Nothing is uploaded to any server.

---

## 🚀 Quick Setup Guide

Follow these 4 simple steps to calibrate your 401(k) deferral plan for the year:

```
[ Step 1: Limit & Age ] ➔ [ Step 2: Salary & Pay Schedule ] ➔ [ Step 3: Match & Bonus ] ➔ [ Step 4: Follow 4-Phase Plan ]
```

### Step 1: Set Your Annual Federal Contribution Limit
1.  Under **Plan Year & Federal Limit Mode**, the standard IRS elective deferral limit for **2026 ($24,500)** is active by default.
2.  If you qualify for **Catch-Up Contributions**, select your age bracket:
    *   **Standard (Under 50)**: `$24,500`
    *   **Age 50–59 Catch-Up**: `$32,500` (adds `$8,000` standard catch-up)
    *   **Age 60–63 Enhanced Catch-Up**: `$36,000` (adds `$11,500` under SECURE 2.0)
3.  If your plan year or limit differs, click **Manual Override** to type any custom dollar amount.

### Step 2: Input Pay Frequency and Annual Salary
1.  Select your **Pay Frequency**:
    *   **Bi-weekly (26 Paychecks)** — Default for most US corporate and tech employers.
    *   **Semi-monthly (24 Paychecks)** — Paid twice a month (e.g., 15th and last day).
    *   **Weekly (52 Paychecks)** — Paid every week.
    *   **Monthly (12 Paychecks)** — Paid once per month.
    *   **Custom Period Count** — Specify any custom number of checks remaining in your plan year.
2.  Enter your **Annual Base Salary** (e.g., `$120,000`).

### Step 3: Configure Employer Match and Event Toggles
1.  Enter your employer's **Match Threshold %** (e.g., `6.00%` if your company matches up to 6% of your paycheck gross).
2.  *(Optional)* If you receive an annual bonus:
    *   Toggle **Annual Lump-Sum Bonus Payout** to **Enabled**.
    *   Set the **Bonus Check #** (e.g., Check `#6`).
    *   Enter your **Bonus Gross Amount** and your intended **Bonus 401(k) Contribution ($)** (or calculate percentage).
3.  *(Optional)* If you anticipate an annual merit raise:
    *   Toggle **Mid-Year Merit Salary Increase** to **Enabled**.
    *   Enter your **New Annual Salary** and the **Effective Check #**.

### Step 4: Review Your 4-Phase Action Plan
Scroll down to the **Optimal Strategy Recommendation** section:
*   **Phase 1 (Start of Year)**: Set the recommended initial rate in your 401(k) portal at the start of January.
*   **Phase 2 (Change Window)**: Shows the exact calendar window to log into your brokerage portal after your bonus/merit check pays.
*   **Phase 3 (Calibrated Glide)**: Switch to the calibrated post-bonus rate for your mid-year checks.
*   **Phase 4 (Final Step-Up)**: For whole-integer plans, step up your rate for the final checks to close out the remaining balance and hit the exact federal limit.

## 📊 Understanding the Schedule Table

The interactive **Period-by-Period Contribution Schedule** below the action plan displays all 26 checks in full transparency:

| Column | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Period** | Check number with contextual badges (`BONUS`, `MERIT ↑`, `ACTION ↓`, `⚡ STEP-UP`, `🎯 MAXED`). |
| **Gross Pay** | Gross earnings for that specific paycheck. |
| **Gross Cumulative** | Year-to-date running total of gross earnings. |
| **Match** | Exact dollar match funded by your employer on that paycheck. |
| **Original Target** | Baseline unadjusted deferrals (shows the danger of capping early). |
| **Calibrated Plan** | Your optimized contribution schedule pacing safely to the final check. |
| **Calibrated Cumulative** | Year-to-date running total of your 401(k) contributions hitting the exact cap. |

---

> 💡 **Tip**: Hover your mouse over any badge in the table to view an instant, plain-English explanation of why that adjustment is scheduled.
