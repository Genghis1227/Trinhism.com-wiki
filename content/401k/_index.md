# 401(k) Max & Bonus Calibration Engine Hub

Welcome to the **401(k) Max & Bonus Calibration Engine** documentation space. The 401(k) Optimizer is a high-precision, 100% client-side financial planning tool built for salaried professionals, tech employees, and high earners who want to max out their annual IRS 401(k) elective deferrals without forfeiting valuable employer matching funds.

---

## 💡 What is the 401(k) Optimizer?

The **401(k) Max & Bonus Calibration Engine** is an interactive web application hosted in the Trinhism Financial Suite at [`/app/401k/`](https://trinhism.com/app/401k/). It models your exact bi-weekly, semi-monthly, weekly, or custom payroll calendar across the full plan year, calculating dynamic deferral percentages that account for lump-sum bonus payouts and mid-year merit raises.

It automatically solves the common dilemma where receiving a large bonus or merit increase front-loads your 401(k) contributions, causing you to max out months early and lose thousands of dollars in per-paycheck company match under plans without a true-up provision.

---

## 🌟 Why Use 401(k) Optimizer? (Value Proposition)

Standard 401(k) calculators only divide your annual limit by your salary and ignore bonus withholdings or mid-year salary changes. The 401(k) Optimizer offers:

*   🛡️ **Match Protection Guarantee**: Solves for multi-phase pacing schedules so you contribute at least your employer match threshold on all 26 (or 24/52) paychecks.
*   🎯 **4-Phase Brokerage Action Timeline**: Tells you exactly what rate to set at the start of the year, when to log into your brokerage/payroll portal (e.g., Fidelity, Vanguard, Empower, ADP), and what rate to switch to after your bonus or merit raise processes.
*   ⚡ **Zero-Shortfall Whole-Integer Strategy**: For payroll portals that do not support fractional percentages (e.g., only whole integers like 10% or 11%), the engine calculates a mathematical step-up schedule (e.g., 15% &rarr; 10% &rarr; 11%) to hit the exact federal limit ($24,500) down to the dollar with zero contribution shortfall.
*   🔒 **100% Client-Side Privacy**: None of your compensation or retirement numbers are sent to external servers or stored in the cloud. All data stays strictly inside your local browser cache (`localStorage`) with one-click JSON export/import backups.
*   📊 **Period-by-Period Contribution Schedule**: Full 26-check transparent table with visual status badges, tooltip explanations, cumulative benchmarks, and printable PDF reports.
